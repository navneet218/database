# Batch 01: 20 leads, 22 Sep 2026

Twenty founders and CEOs across India, the UK, Canada and the US. Each one has a trigger from the last 90 days (funding round or new role) and a visible gap between their company's momentum and their own LinkedIn presence.

Scoring, sequence and templates follow `outreach-playbook.md`. Messages are written in Prabal's voice for the Gliped account: short lines, no em dashes, no pitch in the first touch.

## How this batch was built, and what to check before sending

- Source: funding and leadership news from 1 to 20 Sep 2026 (plus two August/July rounds). The CRM lead list wasn't in Drive, so this is a net-new batch. Everyone here was checked by name against the Drive files that were found, but not against the CRM itself.
- Activity pattern: built from each person's publicly indexed LinkedIn posts. A post's date is encoded in its LinkedIn activity ID, so each timeline below is real. The index only catches some posts, though, so "last indexed post" means "at least this recent", not "last post".
- Not visible from here: reactions, comments, follower counts, and who they engage with. That means intent signals (section A of the playbook) are unscored. If someone views your profile or engages with a Gliped post, add +30 and move them to hot.
- Only the post titles and dates were visible, not the post text. Where a note says [one line on ...], read that post first and write the line yourself. Never praise a post you haven't read.
- Before sending each opener, take 30 seconds on their `/recent-activity/all/` page and confirm three things: still in the role, the last post date, and whether a ghostwriter or agency is already visible. Adjust the hook if they posted in the last week.

## Shared sequence for this batch

Adapted from the 21-day sequence in the playbook, with the follow-up rhythm from the daily-outreach skill.

| Day | Action |
|---|---|
| 1 to 5 | Follow them. Leave 1 or 2 real comments. Most of this batch posts rarely, so comment on their funding post or on the company page's announcement. If there's nothing recent, just view the profile and move on. |
| 6 | Connection note (below). |
| On accept | First DM (below). It asks one question and makes no pitch. |
| +3 to 4 days, no reply | **Follow-up 1:** offer the free 3-point profile teardown. |
| +5 to 6 days after that | **Follow-up 2:** a last nudge that leaves the door open. |
| If they say yes to the teardown | Send a 2 to 3 minute Loom within 24 hours covering the 3 points in their card, then ask for a 20-minute call. |

**Follow-up 1 (same for everyone, fill the bracket):**
> No worries if this is a busy stretch, [Name].
>
> I put together 3 quick notes on your profile.
> Things I'd change so people who hear about [Company] and look you up land on something that sells it.
>
> Want me to send them over? Takes 2 minutes to watch.

**Follow-up 2:**
> Last one from me, [Name].
>
> If LinkedIn moves up the list after the raise settles, I'm around.
> Either way, good luck with [the next milestone].

Time zones: send India leads 9 to 11am IST, UK 8 to 10am UK time, Canada and US East 8 to 10am ET, US West 8 to 10am PT. Tuesday to Thursday.

Limits: 20 connection requests this week is well within safe range. Send by hand.
